Privacy Policy for Drop Merge

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to Drop Merge! We are committed to protecting your privacy and delivering a transparent, safe, and enjoyable casual puzzle experience. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you download, install, or play Drop Merge on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Drop Merge does not require a registration form, password, or sensitive personal data such as your real name, address, contacts, or phone number.

A. Gameplay & Progress Data

  • Local Storage: Your current level progress, high scores, stars, booster inventory (Hammers, Bombs, Undos), coins balance, and achievements are stored locally on your device via encrypted SQLite database storage.
  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud save or sign into Google Play Games, your monotonic game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, high score) is securely synchronized to Firebase Firestore so you can restore your journey across devices.

B. Device & Diagnostic Telemetry

  • Anonymous Analytics: We collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic events (such as level completion/fail rates, booster usage frequency, and frame performance) to balance level difficulty and optimize 60 FPS gameplay.
  • Crash Reports: In the rare event of a technical crash, anonymous technical stack traces and hardware identifiers (device model, Android OS version) are processed strictly to troubleshoot and fix bugs.

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To provide core game features, advertising, and secure purchases, Drop Merge integrates reputable industry-standard SDKs from Google:

  • Google AdMob: We display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may process your device's Google Advertising ID and coarse IP-derived geography to deliver relevant ads and detect fraud. We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR and privacy consent in applicable regions (such as the EEA and UK).
  • Google Play In-App Billing: All store transactions (purchasing coins, booster packs, or the permanent "Remove Ads" entitlement) are handled entirely through Google Play's secure payment infrastructure. We never collect, process, or store your credit card or financial information.
  • Google Play Games Services: Used for optional GamerTag display, leaderboards (Global High Score, Stars, Daily Challenges), and cloud achievement syncing.
  • Firebase Firestore: Provides lightweight, serverless cloud save backup for game progress tied to your authenticated Google Play Games identity.

3. Advertising Choices & Ad Removal

We respect your preferences regarding advertisements:

  • Remove Ads: Players can permanently remove all interstitial and banner advertisements at any time via the in-game Shop ("Remove Ads"). Rewarded video ads remain completely optional for extra moves or bonuses.
  • Consent Management: Players in the EEA/UK can review, manage, or revoke their advertising consent choices at any time from the in-game Settings screen.

4. Data Retention and Player Deletion Rights

Your Right to Delete Data: You have full control over your cloud data. At any time, you can permanently delete your synced cloud progress by tapping "Delete Cloud Save" in the game's Settings screen. You can also reset local data by clearing the app cache/storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Drop Merge is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can immediately delete such data.

6. Security

We implement standard security measures, HTTPS/TLS data transfer protocols, and strict Firebase security rules to protect your game progression against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform policy changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will indicate the latest revision.
