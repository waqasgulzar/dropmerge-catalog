Privacy Policy for Drop Merge

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Welcome to Drop Merge! This privacy policy explains how our mobile game collects, uses, and protects information when you play our game on Android.

1. Information We Collect

Drop Merge is designed to respect your privacy. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information (such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

  • Local Gameplay Data: High scores, level completion progress, earned stars, unlocked trophies, and booster inventory are saved locally on your device using secure SQLite storage.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic telemetry and crash logs may be processed to identify game performance issues and improve stability.

2. Advertising & Monetization

Drop Merge displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These advertising services may collect and process anonymous advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Store policies and GDPR/CCPA guidelines.

Players can opt out of personalized ads or remove banner/interstitial advertisements via the in-game Shop option ("Remove Ads").

3. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases (such as booster packs and Remove Ads) are processed securely through the Google Play In-App Billing system. We never receive or store your credit card or financial information.

4. Children's Privacy

Drop Merge is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or developer contact page.
